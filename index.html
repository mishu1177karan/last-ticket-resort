How it works and house rules

  • We don't buy or hold tickets. We match a buyer's quote with a seller's quote and coordinate the handover in person.
  • Your price stays private. A deal happens when a buyer's maximum is at or above a seller's ask. Buyers pay no more than their maximum, sellers receive at least their ask, and we keep the difference as our margin.
  • Sending a quote is free.
  • Transfer tickets through the booking app's official transfer option where it exists, so the ticket lands in the buyer's account.
  • Sell at or near face value. We may decline quotes priced far above face value.
  • For private bus tickets, check that the operator allows a change of passenger name before you list.
  • Train and flight tickets are tied to the passenger's name and can't be traded here.
